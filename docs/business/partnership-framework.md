# FluoriVenger Partnership Framework

## Executive Summary

FluoriVenger's success depends on strategic partnerships across the value chain—from material sourcing to distribution and impact measurement. This framework outlines partnership models, terms, and mutual value creation opportunities.

## 1. Partnership Philosophy

### 1.1 Core Principles

**Mutual Value Creation**
- Win-win relationships
- Shared goals and metrics
- Transparent communication
- Long-term commitment

**Local First**
- Prioritize Kenyan partners
- Support local economic development
- Build local capacity
- Create local jobs

**Impact Driven**
- Health and environmental outcomes
- Measurable social impact
- Community empowerment
- Sustainable development

**Innovation & Collaboration**
- Co-creation of solutions
- Knowledge sharing
- Continuous improvement
- Adaptive partnerships

## 2. Partnership Categories

### 2.1 Material Supply Partners

#### Kenya Meat Commission (KMC)
**Partnership Type:** Strategic Material Supply

**Current Status:** MOU signed, pilot phase complete

**Value Exchange:**
- **FluoriVenger receives:**
  - Bone material (500+ kg/week)
  - Consistent quality and supply
  - Preferential pricing
  - Technical support for processing

- **KMC receives:**
  - Waste disposal solution (saves KES 50-100/ton)
  - Revenue from waste material (KES 0.50/kg)
  - Enhanced sustainability profile
  - CSR impact (community health)

**Agreement Terms:**
- Duration: 5 years (renewable)
- Exclusivity: First right of refusal on bone material
- Minimum volume: 500 kg/week (Year 1) → 2,000 kg/week (Year 5)
- Price: KES 0.50/kg (fixed for 2 years, then CPI-adjusted)
- Quality standards: Defined specifications (see Appendix A)
- Payment terms: Net 30 days
- Force majeure: Standard provisions

**Performance Metrics:**
- Supply consistency: >95% on-time delivery
- Quality compliance: >98% meeting specifications
- Volume growth: 20-30% YoY
- Cost savings: KES 2-5M/year for KMC

**Governance:**
- Quarterly business reviews
- Joint technical committee
- Annual strategic planning
- Dispute resolution: Mediation, then arbitration

#### County Abattoirs Network
**Partnership Type:** Decentralized Material Supply

**Target Partners:** 20+ county abattoirs across Rift Valley

**Value Exchange:**
- **FluoriVenger receives:**
  - Distributed bone supply
  - Reduced logistics costs
  - Local market access
  - Community relationships

- **Abattoirs receive:**
  - Waste disposal solution
  - Additional revenue (KES 0.30-0.50/kg)
  - Technical training
  - Equipment support (if needed)

**Agreement Terms:**
- Duration: 3 years (renewable)
- Non-exclusive: Multiple suppliers per county
- Minimum volume: 100 kg/week per abattoir
- Price: KES 0.30-0.50/kg (based on volume and quality)
- Quality standards: Same as KMC
- Payment terms: Net 15 days

**Implementation Plan:**
- Phase 1 (Year 1): 5 abattoirs (Baringo, Nakuru)
- Phase 2 (Year 2): 10 additional abattoirs
- Phase 3 (Year 3+): 20+ abattoirs across region

#### Clay Suppliers
**Partnership Type:** Raw Material Supply

**Target Partners:** Local clay miners in Kajiado, Nakuru

**Value Exchange:**
- **FluoriVenger receives:**
  - High-quality montmorillonite clay
  - Consistent supply (300+ kg/week)
  - Competitive pricing

- **Suppliers receive:**
  - Guaranteed offtake
  - Premium pricing for quality
  - Long-term contracts
  - Technical specifications support

**Agreement Terms:**
- Duration: 3 years
- Price: KES 2-3/kg (quality-based)
- Quality standards: CEC >70 meq/100g, particle size <0.5mm
- Payment terms: Net 30 days

**Model 3: Joint Venture (Community Kiosks)**

- Shared investment and risk
- Profit sharing: 50/50

- Co-marketing budget: KES 2-5M/year (shared)
- Exclusive partnership in water filtration sector
- Joint product development
- Quarterly business reviews

**Target Outcomes:**
- 500+ integrated systems (Year 1-3)
- KES 50M+ in combined revenue
- 50+ joint kiosk installations
- Market leadership in water-energy solutions

### 2.3 Distribution & Market Access Partners

#### County Governments
**Partnership Type:** Public Health & Distribution

**Target Partners:** 10+ Rift Valley county governments

**Value Exchange:**
- **FluoriVenger receives:**
  - Market access and credibility
  - Bulk procurement contracts
  - Health facility partnerships
  - Community mobilization support

- **Counties receive:**
  - Affordable fluoride removal solution
  - Reduced healthcare burden
  - SDG 6 achievement (clean water)
  - Job creation and economic development

**Partnership Models:**

**Model 1: Bulk Procurement**
- County purchases filters for distribution
- Subsidized pricing: 20-30% discount
- Target: Schools, health facilities, public institutions
- Volume: 1,000-5,000 units per county per year

**Model 2: Public-Private Partnership (PPP)**
- Joint investment in community kiosks
- County provides land and water source
- FluoriVenger provides equipment and operations
- Revenue sharing: 60% FluoriVenger, 40% County

**Model 3: Health Program Integration**
- Filters distributed through health facilities
- Fluorosis screening and monitoring
- Health education campaigns
- Impact measurement and reporting

**Agreement Terms:**
- Memorandum of Understanding (MOU)
- Duration: 3-5 years
- Pricing: Bulk discounts (20-30%)
- Payment terms: Net 60-90 days (government)
- Performance metrics: Health outcomes, coverage
- Reporting: Quarterly impact reports

**Implementation Roadmap:**
- Year 1: 3 pilot counties (Baringo, Nakuru, Narok)
- Year 2: 5 additional counties
- Year 3+: Regional expansion (10+ counties)

**Success Metrics:**
- Households reached: 10,000+ per county
- Fluorosis reduction: 50%+ in 3 years
- Healthcare cost savings: KES 20-50M per county
- Job creation: 50-100 per county

#### Microfinance Institutions (MFIs)
**Partnership Type:** Consumer Financing

**Target Partners:**
- Kenya Women Microfinance Bank (KWFT)
- Faulu Microfinance
- SMEP Microfinance
- SACCOs (Savings and Credit Cooperatives)

**Value Exchange:**
- **FluoriVenger receives:**
  - Increased affordability for customers
  - Larger addressable market
  - Reduced credit risk
  - Faster sales cycles

- **MFIs receive:**
  - New loan product (water filters)
  - Social impact lending
  - Low default rates (essential product)
  - Cross-selling opportunities

**Financing Models:**

**Model 1: Direct Lending**
- MFI provides loans to customers
- Loan amount: KES 600-5,000
- Term: 6-12 months
- Interest rate: 12-18% per annum
- FluoriVenger: No financial risk

**Model 2: Vendor Financing**
- FluoriVenger provides credit to customers
- MFI provides working capital to FluoriVenger
- Interest rate: 10-15% per annum
- FluoriVenger: Manages credit risk

**Model 3: Lease-to-Own**
- MFI owns filters, leases to customers
- Monthly payment: KES 100-150
- Ownership transfer after 12 months
- FluoriVenger: Buyback guarantee

**Agreement Terms:**
- Partnership duration: 3 years
- Target: 30-40% of household sales via financing
- Default rate target: <5%
- FluoriVenger guarantee: 5-10% of loan value
- Commission: 2-5% of loan value to FluoriVenger

**Risk Mitigation:**
- Customer screening and credit checks
- Product registration and tracking
- Repossession rights (lease-to-own)
- Insurance options

## 7. Partnership Governance

### 7.1 Governance Structure

**Executive Steering Committee**
- Senior leaders from both organizations
- Quarterly meetings
- Strategic decisions and oversight
- Conflict escalation

**Operational Management Team**
- Day-to-day partnership management
- Monthly meetings
- Performance monitoring
- Issue resolution

**Working Groups**
- Specific initiatives or projects
- As-needed meetings
- Cross-functional collaboration
- Deliverable-focused

### 7.2 Decision-Making Framework

**Level 1: Operational Decisions**
- Made by operational teams
- Within agreed parameters
- No escalation needed
- Examples: Scheduling, routine orders

**Level 2: Tactical Decisions**
- Made by management team
- Moderate impact and risk
- Consultation with executives
- Examples: Pricing adjustments, process changes

**Level 3: Strategic Decisions**
- Made by steering committee
- High impact and risk
- Formal approval process
- Examples: Contract amendments, major investments

### 7.3 Dispute Resolution Process

**Step 1: Direct Negotiation**
- Parties attempt to resolve directly
- Timeline: 7 days
- Documentation of issues and attempts

**Step 2: Mediation**
- Neutral third-party mediator
- Timeline: 14 days
- Non-binding recommendations

**Step 3: Arbitration**
- Binding arbitration
- Timeline: 30 days
- Enforceable decision

**Step 4: Legal Action**
- Last resort
- Governed by agreement terms
- Kenyan law jurisdiction
