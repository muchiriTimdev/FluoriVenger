# FluoriVenger Bio-Mineral Filter Technical Specifications

## Executive Summary

The FluoriVenger filter system uses locally-sourced bone char, activated clay, and carbonized maize cobs to remove fluoride from drinking water. This document provides comprehensive technical specifications for manufacturing, testing, and deploying the filter system.

## 1. Filter Composition

### 1.1 Material Breakdown

| Material | Percentage | Function | Source |
|----------|-----------|----------|--------|
| Bone Char | 40% | Primary fluoride adsorbent | Kenya Meat Commission abattoirs |
| Activated Clay | 30% | Secondary filtration, structural integrity | Local clay deposits (Kajiado, Nakuru) |
| Carbonized Maize Cobs | 20% | Additional adsorption, flow regulation | Agricultural waste |
| Natural Binders | 10% | Structural binding, food-grade safety | Cassava starch, guar gum |

### 1.2 Bone Char Specifications

**Chemical Composition:**
- Hydroxyapatite [Ca₁₀(PO₄)₆(OH)₂]: 70-80%
- Carbon: 10-15%
- Trace minerals: 5-10%

**Physical Properties:**
- Particle size: 0.5-2.0 mm (after crushing)
- Surface area: 90-120 m²/g
- Porosity: 40-50%
- Bulk density: 0.6-0.8 g/cm³

**Processing Requirements:**
1. Collection from abattoirs (cattle, goat bones preferred)
2. Cleaning and degreasing (hot water wash)
3. Carbonization at 600-800°C for 4-6 hours
4. Crushing and sieving to 0.5-2.0 mm
5. Acid wash (0.1M HCl) to activate surface
6. Rinse until pH neutral (6.5-7.5)
7. Drying at 105°C for 24 hours

### 1.3 Activated Clay Specifications

**Clay Type:** Montmorillonite-rich bentonite

**Physical Properties:**
- Particle size: 0.1-0.5 mm
- Surface area: 200-300 m²/g
- Cation exchange capacity: 70-100 meq/100g
- pH: 7.0-8.5

**Activation Process:**
1. Raw clay collection and drying
2. Grinding to <0.5 mm
3. Acid activation (2M H₂SO₄, 4 hours at 90°C)
4. Washing until pH 6.5-7.5
5. Drying at 110°C for 12 hours

### 1.4 Carbonized Maize Cob Specifications

**Physical Properties:**
- Particle size: 1-3 mm
- Carbon content: 75-85%
- Surface area: 300-500 m²/g
- Ash content: <10%

**Carbonization Process:**
1. Collection and drying of maize cobs
2. Pyrolysis at 400-500°C for 2-3 hours (limited oxygen)
3. Cooling and crushing to 1-3 mm
4. Sieving and quality control

## 2. Filter Cartridge Design

### 2.1 Physical Dimensions

**Standard Household Cartridge:**
- Height: 250 mm
- Diameter: 100 mm
- Wall thickness: 5 mm
- Volume capacity: 1.5 liters
- Weight (dry): 1.2 kg
- Weight (saturated): 2.7 kg

**Community Kiosk Cartridge:**
- Height: 500 mm
- Diameter: 200 mm
- Wall thickness: 8 mm
- Volume capacity: 12 liters
- Weight (dry): 9.5 kg
- Weight (saturated): 21.5 kg

### 2.2 Cartridge Construction

**Housing Material:** Food-grade HDPE (High-Density Polyethylene)
- UV stabilized
- BPA-free
- Temperature range: 5-50°C
- Pressure rating: 0.5-2.0 bar

**Layer Structure (Top to Bottom):**
1. **Pre-filter layer** (20mm): Coarse gravel (2-5mm) - removes sediment
2. **Primary filtration layer** (100mm): Bio-mineral mix (bone char, clay, maize cob)
3. **Secondary filtration layer** (50mm): Fine sand (0.5-1mm) - polishing
4. **Support layer** (30mm): Coarse gravel (5-10mm) - structural support
5. **Drainage layer** (20mm): Perforated base plate

**Flow Direction:** Top-down (gravity-fed) or bottom-up (pressure-fed)

### 2.3 Connection Fittings

- **Inlet:** 1/2" BSP threaded connection
- **Outlet:** 1/2" BSP threaded connection
- **Bypass valve:** Optional for backwashing
- **Pressure gauge port:** 1/4" NPT
- **Sampling port:** 1/4" quick-connect

## 3. Performance Specifications

### 3.1 Fluoride Removal Efficiency

**Target Performance:**
- Input fluoride concentration: 2-15 mg/L
- Output fluoride concentration: <1.5 mg/L (WHO guideline)
- Removal efficiency: 85-95%

**Performance by Input Concentration:**

| Input (mg/L) | Output (mg/L) | Removal (%) | Lifespan Impact |
|--------------|---------------|-------------|-----------------|
| 2-4 | 0.3-0.8 | 85-90% | 12 months |
| 4-8 | 0.5-1.2 | 88-93% | 9 months |
| 8-12 | 0.8-1.4 | 90-95% | 6 months |
| 12-15 | 1.0-1.5 | 92-95% | 4 months |

### 3.2 Flow Rate

**Household Cartridge:**
- Gravity-fed: 2-3 L/min
- Pressure-fed (0.5 bar): 3-4 L/min
- Pressure-fed (1.0 bar): 4-5 L/min

**Community Kiosk Cartridge:**
- Gravity-fed: 8-12 L/min
- Pressure-fed (0.5 bar): 12-16 L/min
- Pressure-fed (1.0 bar): 16-20 L/min

### 3.3 Filter Lifespan

**Household Cartridge (Family of 5, 20L/day):**
- Low fluoride (2-4 mg/L): 12 months / 7,200 liters
- Medium fluoride (4-8 mg/L): 9 months / 5,400 liters
- High fluoride (8-15 mg/L): 6 months / 3,600 liters

**Community Kiosk Cartridge (200L/day):**
- Low fluoride: 12 months / 72,000 liters
- Medium fluoride: 9 months / 54,000 liters
- High fluoride: 6 months / 36,000 liters

**End-of-Life Indicators:**
- Fluoride breakthrough (output >1.5 mg/L)
- Flow rate reduction >50%
- Visible discoloration or fouling
- Pressure drop increase >100%

### 3.4 Additional Contaminant Removal

| Contaminant | Removal Efficiency | Notes |
|-------------|-------------------|-------|
| Turbidity | 90-95% | Pre-filter + clay |
| Bacteria (E. coli) | 99.9% | Adsorption + filtration |
| Heavy metals (Pb, Cd) | 70-85% | Ion exchange with bone char |
| Organic matter | 60-75% | Carbonized maize cob |
| Hardness (Ca, Mg) | 20-30% | Partial ion exchange |

## 4. Manufacturing Process

### 4.1 Material Preparation Timeline

**Week 1-2: Material Collection**
- Bone collection from abattoirs (500 kg/batch)
- Clay mining and transport (300 kg/batch)
- Maize cob collection (200 kg/batch)

**Week 3: Material Processing**
- Day 1-2: Bone cleaning and degreasing
- Day 3-4: Bone carbonization (600-800°C)
- Day 5: Clay acid activation
- Day 6: Maize cob carbonization (400-500°C)
- Day 7: Crushing, sieving, quality control

**Week 4: Cartridge Assembly**
- Day 1-2: Material mixing and homogenization
- Day 3-4: Cartridge filling and compaction
- Day 5: Sealing and fitting installation
- Day 6-7: Quality testing and packaging

### 4.2 Quality Control Checkpoints

**Raw Material QC:**
1. Bone char: Surface area, particle size, pH
2. Clay: CEC, particle size, moisture content
3. Maize cob char: Carbon content, ash content

**In-Process QC:**
1. Mix homogeneity: Visual inspection, sampling
2. Compaction density: Weight per volume
3. Layer integrity: Visual inspection

**Final Product QC:**
1. Fluoride removal test (lab conditions)
2. Flow rate measurement
3. Pressure drop test
4. Leak test (pressure hold)
5. Microbial safety test

## 5. Installation and Maintenance

### 5.1 Installation Requirements

**Household System:**
1. Clean water source (borehole, tap, rainwater)
2. Mounting bracket or stand (included)
3. Input/output piping (1/2" HDPE or PVC)
4. Storage container (20-50 liters)
5. Optional: Pressure pump for low-flow sources

**Community Kiosk System:**
1. Water source with minimum 0.5 bar pressure
2. Pre-filtration (sediment filter 50 micron)
3. Pressure tank (200-500 liters)
4. Distribution taps (4-6 outlets)
5. Solar pump system (D&S integration)
6. Monitoring system (IoT sensors)

### 5.2 Maintenance Schedule

**Weekly:**
- Visual inspection for leaks
- Flow rate check
- Outlet water quality spot check

**Monthly:**
- Fluoride test (field test kit)
- Backwash (if applicable)
- Pressure gauge check
- Clean pre-filter

**Quarterly:**
- Comprehensive water quality test
- System performance evaluation
- Replace pre-filter if needed

**Annually or End-of-Life:**
- Replace filter cartridge
- System deep clean
- Recalibrate monitoring sensors

### 5.3 Backwashing Procedure (Optional)

For systems with backwash capability:
1. Close inlet valve
2. Open backwash valve
3. Reverse flow for 5-10 minutes
4. Drain backwash water
5. Resume normal operation
6. Test output water quality

**Frequency:** Every 2-4 weeks for high-turbidity sources

## 6. Testing and Validation

### 6.1 Laboratory Testing Protocol

**Fluoride Analysis:**
- Method: Ion-selective electrode (ISE) or Ion chromatography (IC)
- Standard: APHA 4500-F
- Frequency: Every batch, monthly field samples

**Microbial Testing:**
- Method: Membrane filtration
- Parameters: Total coliforms, E. coli
- Standard: APHA 9222
- Frequency: Quarterly

**Physical-Chemical Parameters:**
- pH, turbidity, TDS, hardness
- Standard: APHA methods
- Frequency: Monthly

### 6.2 Field Testing Protocol

**Fluoride Field Test:**
- Method: SPADNS colorimetric test kit
- Range: 0-2.0 mg/L
- Accuracy: ±0.1 mg/L
- Frequency: Weekly (community kiosks), monthly (households)

**Flow Rate Test:**
- Method: Timed volume collection
- Equipment: Stopwatch, graduated container
- Frequency: Monthly
