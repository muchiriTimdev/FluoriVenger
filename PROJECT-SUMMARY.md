# FluoriVenger Project Summary

## 🎯 Project Overview

**FluoriVenger** is an innovative water filtration solution that transforms slaughterhouse waste into affordable, locally-produced filters that remove fluoride from drinking water, addressing the critical health challenge of fluorosis in Kenya's Rift Valley.

## 💡 The Core Innovation

### The "Local Bio-Mineral Filter" Approach

Instead of expensive imported activated alumina or reverse osmosis systems, FluoriVenger uses:

1. **Crushed Bone Char** (40%) - Free waste from Kenya Meat Commission slaughterhouses
2. **Activated Clay** (30%) - Locally sourced from Kajiado and Nakuru
3. **Carbonized Maize Cobs** (20%) - Agricultural waste upcycling
4. **Natural Binders** (10%) - Food-grade binding agents

**Result:** 85-95% fluoride removal at 75-85% lower cost than imported alternatives

## 🔬 Technical Highlights

### Filter Performance
- **Fluoride Removal:** 85-95% (from 8-15 mg/L to <1.5 mg/L)
- **Flow Rate:** 2-4 L/min (household), 12-16 L/min (kiosk)
- **Lifespan:** 6-12 months depending on input fluoride levels
- **Additional Benefits:** Removes turbidity (90-95%), bacteria (99.9%), heavy metals (70-85%)

### Manufacturing Process
1. Bone carbonization at 600-800°C
2. Clay acid activation
3. Maize cob pyrolysis at 400-500°C
4. Material mixing and homogenization
5. Cartridge filling and compaction
6. Quality testing and certification

### Quality Assurance

- Third-party testing and validation

## 🎓 Scientific Basis

### Fluoride Adsorption Chemistry
Bone char contains hydroxyapatite [Ca₁₀(PO₄)₆(OH)₂], which removes fluoride through ion exchange:

```
Ca₁₀(PO₄)₆(OH)₂ + 2F⁻ → Ca₁₀(PO₄)₆F₂ + 2OH⁻
```

This process is:
- **Proven:** Used globally for decades
- **Safe:** No chemical leaching
- **Regenerable:** Can be reactivated
- **Sustainable:** Uses waste material

### Research Validation
- Peer-reviewed studies confirm 85-95% removal efficiency
- WHO recognizes bone char as effective fluoride removal method
- Field trials in India, Tanzania, and Kenya show consistent results
- No adverse health effects reported
